Organisation Name: Baked by B 

Baked by B was founded from a passion for baking and a love for creating desserts that bring people 
together. What started as baking for family and friends soon grew into a small home-based bakery 
after the quality, creativity, and taste of the baked treats received positive feedback. Today, Baked by 
B specialises in freshly baked cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and a variety of sweet desserts for birthdays, 
weddings, baby showers, graduations, and many other special occasions. Every order is carefully 
prepared using quality ingredients, with the aim of making every celebration a little sweeter and every 
customers feel valued 

Mission Statement 

The mission of Baked by B is to create fresh, high-quality baked goods that bring joy to every 
celebration. We are committed to using quality ingredients, providing excellent customer service, and 
creating beautifully crafted treats that make every customer's special occasion memorable. 

Vision Statement 

The vision of Baked by B is to become a trusted and well-known bakery recognised for creativity, 
quality, and exceptional customer service. We aim to grow our business while continuing to make 
every celebration sweeter with delicious and beautifully crafted baked goods. 

Target Audience 

Baked by B is designed for individuals, families, and businesses seeking beautifully crafted, high
quality baked goods for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, corporate events, and other 
memorable occasions. The bakery welcomes customers of all ages who appreciate fresh, delicious 
desserts made with care. 

Website Goals and Objectives 

The main goal of the Baked by B website is to create a professional online presence where customers 
can discover the bakery, explore the wide range of freshly baked products, view special offers, learn 
about the services available and easily find contact information. The website will provide customers 
with a simple and enjoyable way to browse the bakery's products and make enquiries or place orders. 
The website will also help increase brand awareness, attract new customers, build trust, and 
strengthen relationships with existing customers. The success of the website will be measured by the 
number of website visitors, customer enquiries, orders received, and returning customers. An increase 
in these areas will indicate that the website is successfully supporting the growth of Baked by B. 

Current Website Analysis 

Baked by B currently operates without an official website. Customers place orders mainly through 
social media platforms and WhatsApp. While these platforms are useful for communication, they do 
not provide customers with one place to view products, learn about the bakery, or access important 
information. An official website will improve the business's online presence and make it easier for 
both new and existing customers to interact with the bakery. 

Strengths 

• Freshly baked, homemade products made with quality ingredients. 

• Custom cakes and desserts for a variety of occasions. 

• Friendly, reliable, and personalised customer service.  

• Strong customer relationships through social media and WhatsApp.  

Weaknesses 

• No official website to showcase the bakery.  

• Customers cannot easily browse the full product range. 

• Limited online information about products, prices, and services.  

• New customers may find it difficult to learn about the business.  

Areas for Improvement

Developing an official website will provide customers with one convenient place to explore Baked by 
B. Visitors will be able to browse products, view cake designs, learn more about the bakery, discover 
special offers, place enquiries, and find contact information quickly and easily. The website will also 
strengthen the bakery's online presence, improve customer experience, and help attract new customers 
while supporting the continued growth of the business. 

Proposed Website Features and Functionality 

The Baked by B website will be designed to provide customers with an easy and enjoyable 
browsing experience. Each page will have a clear purpose, allowing visitors to quickly find 
the information they need and learn more about the bakery's products and services. 

• Home 

The Home page will welcome visitors to Baked by B and introduce the bakery. It will 
showcase featured baked goods, highlight the bakery's passion for quality, and encourage 
visitors to explore the rest of the website. 

• About Us 

The About Us page will share the story of Baked by B, including the bakery's history, 
mission, vision, and values. This page will help customers learn more about the business and 
build trust in the brand.

• Products 

The Products page will display a selection of freshly baked cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and 
other desserts. Each product category will include images, descriptions, and starting prices to 
help customers choose the perfect treats for their special occasions. 

• Special Offers 

The Special Offers page will keep customers informed about current promotions, seasonal 
discounts, and limited time deals available at the bakery. 

• Custom Orders 

The Custom Orders page will explain how customers can request personalised cakes and 
desserts for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, and other celebrations. It will 
also provide information about the ordering process and customisation options. 

• Contact Us 

The Contact Us page will include the bakery's contact details, social media links, business 
hours, and a contact form where customers can send enquiries or request more information.

Website Functionality 

The website will include a navigation menu on every page, making it easy for customers to 
move around the website. High-quality product images and detailed descriptions will help 
visitors explore the bakery's offerings, while clear contact information will make it simple to 
place orders or ask questions. The website will be designed to provide a user-friendly 
experience and encourage customers to return. 

Design and User Experience 

The Baked by B website will have a simple, clean, and easy-to-use design. Each page will have clear 
headings, readable text, and high-quality images to help customers learn more about the bakery and its 
products. The website will use a consistent layout on every page to make browsing simple and 
organised. A navigation menu will appear at the top of every page, allowing visitors to move easily 
between the Home, About Us, Products, Special Offers, Custom Orders, and Contact Us pages. 
Product images and descriptions will help customers explore the bakery's products, while the simple 
layout will ensure that information is easy to find and understand. 

Technical Requirements 

The Baked by B website will be developed using HTML in Visual Studio Code. The website 
will consist of multiple linked web pages, images, and a navigation menu to allow users to 
move easily between pages. If the business decides to publish the website online in the future, 
a domain name and web hosting service will be required.

To gain more ideas for the proposed baked by b website, I analysed a similar website called Tessa's

request custom cakes and find contact information. The website also tells the bakery story, helping 
people viewing the website connect more with the brand. (Tessa's Bakery, n.d.)
